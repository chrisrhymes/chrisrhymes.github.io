Rodger pulled the chair back from under the desk, then nodded at Lily, indicating that she should sit down. She politely followed the instruction, placing her laptop bag under the desk. He stepped to the side and pressed the power button on the monitor to turn it on. The screen flashed to life, displaying a login screen. No fancy logo, just the boxes for username and password and a login button.

Reaching into his jacket pocket, Rodger retrieved a small envelope, handing it to Lily. "Here is your badge and your credentials for the system with a temporary password. You will be asked for a new one once you have logged in for the first time. You'll notice there is no 'reset password' option, so if you forget it, then you need to speak to one of our staff in person."

Rodger walked around the far side of the desk and turned slightly as he spoke, pointing his hands in the direction of the areas he was talking about. "Over there is the kitchen. Great coffee, but it's pretty strong so take it steady. Toilets are over there. The desks around the room have been split into sections for each Government agency. Normally the more the desks, the more the funding."

Lily looked at the desks around the edge of the room and noticed for the first time the small signs indicating the name of the agencies each desk belonged to. The usual suspects were there, Department of Defence, RAF, Army, Navy, MI5 and MI6. She wondered what happened to MIs 1 to 4. She then looked at the desks nearest her. She was the only person sitting at a desk with the label of National Organisation for Space and Extraterrestrial Exploration. The lack of desks, and therefore, lack of budget allocation, had helped explain why their agency was based in a small room within a university campus and the fact there were only two of them that worked there.

Rodger continued his tour. "Up there, where you came in, is the way out. There is also an emergency exit on the opposite site of the room. Lets just hope we don't have to use it as there are lots and lots of stairs to get back up to ground level." Rodger smiled at Lily as he spoke the last part.

His brief smile quickly dropped back to a solemn glance. "Ah, but of course, you won't be able to leave until we have resolved this situation we find ourselves in." He looked up at the clock displaying on the large video wall and Lily followed his gaze. It was 09:30:33 already.

"So what exactly do you want from me?" Lily asked for clarification, the panic slowly starting to creep back into her voice.

Rodger nodded his head gently, then began his explanation. "At 8:30am this morning, a large energy signature was detected outside the offices of Rubhock Industries, just outside Hounslow, London. The public has been fed a story of a gas leak, but all other agencies are at a loss to the origin. One by one all the, how we say, terrestrial agencies have drawn a blank. We wondered if it was an aerial attack, but the RAF have confirmed there were no rogue actors in the vicinity. This left us with one other possible option. Hence calling on the National Organisation for Space and Extraterrestrial Exploration." He held both hands out before him indicating Lily.
"I have to be honest, I was hoping Professor Rang would be able to provide us with a simple explanation and we could all go on with our day, but things don't always work out the way you expect. If they did, then this place wouldn't need to exist. So in answer to your question, we need you to step up to the plate. Tell us what we are dealing with and how concerned we need to be. Then provide us with direction about what precautions we need to take next."

Lily smiled a forced smile at Rodger. "Of course. Anything else?"

"Oh, and we need the update within the next 30 minutes." Rodger nodded, satisfied with himself at the explanation he had provided, then turned and walked towards the kitchen to get himself a coffee. "29 minutes!" He shouted back to Lily, pointing at his watch as he walked.

Lily muttered an expletive under her breath.

Opening the envelope, she retrieved the ID card on a lanyard and placed it over her neck. She glanced down at the photo and name on the ID card. She didn't recognise the person in the photo, but she was pretty sure it wasn't her. An elderly balding man with thick rimmed glasses stared back at her from the ID card. It was Professor Rang. She rolled her eyes. She hurriedly removed the lanyard and placed it and the ID card in her jacket pocket. Next she retrieved the username and password, printed on a small piece of paper. The username was also for Professor Rang. It would have to do, time was against her.

Typing the details exactly as she read them from the piece of paper into the two boxes on screen, she pressed the 'Login' button and waited. A spinner appeared, then five seconds later, a large message in a red font filled the screen. It read "Account suspended. User expired."

She banged her fists down onto the desk in frustration, the sound echoing around the cavernous room. Everyone turned in her direction to see where the source of the noise came from. After the noise had stopped echoing, Lily offered an apology to the room. "Sorry, just some IT issues. Nothing to worry about."

Lily shrunk down in her chair, hiding behind the large computer monitor on her desk. She looked down at the floor in despair. All these world leaders and government agencies were waiting on her to provide them with information about some unknown energy source that all other government agencies had ruled out being related to them. She held her head in her hands, trying not to cry.

Then she saw her laptop bag under the desk. It filled her with a renewed sense of hope. Jake was still at the agency. Maybe he had been able to piece some things together by now. Getting the laptop out of the bag and putting it on the desk, she reached behind the computer terminal on the desk and pulled out the network cable and the display cable, then plugged them both into her own laptop.

Logging back into her NOSEE laptop, she opened up a VPN connection via the network cable and was back into the NOSEE system, then shared her laptop screen onto the large monitor on the desk. Opening her chat client, she sent a message to Jake.

"Rubhock Industries incident. Tell me what you know!" She typed into the chat box as fast as she could, but before she pressed send, she checked the 'urgent' status. The message was sent, but Jake's status was set to 'Away'. Of all the times to go and get a coffee, he had chosen now!

She felt like smashing the table again with her hands, but remembered what happened last time and decided against it. Whilst Jake was away from his desk, she would look through all of their monitoring systems to see if there was any logical explanation for what had happened.
